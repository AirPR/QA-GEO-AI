## Key Components

- **GEO-1550**: This story collects and stores the natural answer; it does not read it (reading = stories 50.2 onward, GEO-1551). The natural mode is switched on **engine by engine** (a setting per engine, off by default, analysis engines only; internal engines always stay structured). Engines are turned on in production only once 50.2 to 50.7 are live, one by one, decided with the PM from the comparison of story 1.4. QA checks it on staging, on a test organisation, with one engine switched on: in the same analysis that engine answers naturally while the others keep today's request. With every engine off, nothing changes. Full answers are kept 2 years, then purged. AC5 (Perception-style reading) and AC6 are delivered by the reader (50.2, 50.6).
- **GEO-1551**: This story builds the reader and produces every field of the output contract. The finer rules land on top of it: brand recognition and the end of the top-10 cap (50.3), ranking details and the 3-run comparison (50.4), competitive outcome columns (50.5), answers not counted (50.6), metrics (50.7), Brand Image reading (50.14). QA checks on staging with one engine switched to natural: brands in order of first mention in the question panel, no brand the answer does not name, sources = the engine's search results on its route (5 for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini; Perplexity returns its full citation list, up to 15; none for Grok). Mention positions and citation numbering have no screen before GEO-1498 and are checked by the technical tests. Quality vs. the labels (target 95% per field) is reported per engine, not a gate.
- **GEO-1552**: Backend only, depends on STORY-50.2 (GEO-1551). Rank by first mention and product rows: STORY-50.4. Share of Voice formula: STORY-50.7. Display: GEO-1498. Engines not switched to natural answers keep today's rules, including the top-10 cap. AC2, AC5 and AC8 depend on what an engine writes on the day: they are checked by the technical ACs, not by QA.
- **GEO-1553**: Backend only, depends on STORY-50.2 (GEO-1551) and STORY-50.3 (GEO-1552). Leaving a no-brand answer out of the Share of Voice denominator is done with the new formula in STORY-50.7; the "No brands named" verdict and the ranking rows of the question panel are in GEO-1498. The 3-run comparison of the old and new methods (5-point tolerance, reported, not a gate) is a report for the PM, run once STORY-50.5 to 50.7 are on staging; it is not a QA check.
- **GEO-1554**: Backend only, depends on STORY-50.2 to 50.4 (GEO-1551, GEO-1552, GEO-1553). This story reads and stores the outcome. AI Choice and every view that reads whether the brand won are STORY-50.7; leaving a not-counted answer out of the metrics is STORY-50.6; the display (AI Choice donut, outcome on engine pills) is GEO-1498. Until GEO-1498, QA checks the stored outcome through the support read.
- **GEO-1555**: Backend only, depends on STORY-50.1 to 50.5 (GEO-1550 to GEO-1554). AC4 is delivered by STORY-50.1 and the full-text reading of AC5 by STORY-50.2; this story adds the failure unit of AC5. The "not counted" display is GEO-1498; the metric formulas (SOV, AI Choice) are STORY-50.7. The rules apply to new analysis dates only: analyses collected before the switch and engines still structured keep today's behaviour.
- **GEO-1556**: Backend only, depends on STORY-50.2 to 50.6 (GEO-1551 to GEO-1555). Every display change named above (AI Choice donut, engine pills, SOV tooltip text, labels, Excel layout) is GEO-1498. AC3 and AC11 to AC15 are delivered by the earlier stories and checked here by tests. Turning Grok's web search on is an Engineering investigation, not part of this story.
