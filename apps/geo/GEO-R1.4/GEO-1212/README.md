## Epic Overview

The EU AI Act (Article 50) applies since 2 Aug 2026: AI-generated content must say so where the user reads it. GEO already shows the statement on the InBrief perception summary, this epic adds the disclosure on the other surfaces that show AI data.

The rule is one disclosure per surface, not one per widget. Every GEO figure is computed from AI engine answers, so one footer covers the whole surface: the Top Topics, Share of Voice, landscape table, Citation Sources, prompt rows and article rows get nothing of their own. The InBrief module keeps its own inline disclaimer ("Generated by artificial intelligence") because it is a generated text, not a measure, and the footer comes on top of it.

Design: Measure - GEO Module, page "AI disclosure", section 01: https://www.figma.com/design/xctSRqtM653zRS7QY3KMm0/Measure---GEO-Module?node-id=17732-1714
