## Description / Functional Need

_Jira description, verbatim._

The EU AI Act (Article 50) applies since 2 Aug 2026: AI-generated content must say so where the user reads it. GEO already shows the statement on the InBrief perception summary, this epic adds the disclosure on the other surfaces that show AI data.

The rule is one disclosure per surface, not one per widget. Every GEO figure is computed from AI engine answers, so one footer covers the whole surface: the Top Topics, Share of Voice, landscape table, Citation Sources, prompt rows and article rows get nothing of their own. The InBrief module keeps its own inline disclaimer ("Generated by artificial intelligence") because it is a generated text, not a measure, and the footer comes on top of it.

Design: Measure - GEO Module, page "AI disclosure", section 01: https://www.figma.com/design/xctSRqtM653zRS7QY3KMm0/Measure---GEO-Module?node-id=17732-1714

---

Component and strings

Web surfaces use the local component GEO / AI Disclosure Footer [1.0], with one form per string key.

| Form | String key | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Long | aiDisclosure.long | Analysis page |
| Short | aiDisclosure.short | Details panels |
| Export | aiDisclosure.export | PowerPoint slides, PDF pages, Excel sheets (same text as the long form, text only) |

| Language | Long and Export | Short |
|---|---|---|
| EN | "Metrics computed with Onclusive's methodology · Summaries generated by artificial intelligence, Powered by AI Sense" | "Generated by artificial intelligence, Powered by AI Sense" |
| FR | "Métriques calculées selon la méthodologie Onclusive · Synthèses générées par intelligence artificielle, propulsé par AI Sense" | "Généré par intelligence artificielle, propulsé par AI Sense" |
| ES | "Métricas calculadas con la metodología de Onclusive · Resúmenes generados por inteligencia artificial, con tecnología de AI Sense" | "Generado por inteligencia artificial, con tecnología de AI Sense" |

"propulsé par" and "con tecnología de" are the words the product already uses next to AI Sense, in lowercase after the comma. "AI Sense" is a name and is never translated.

"AI Sense" always shows highlighted with the AI Sense gradient, as in the design system AI Sense Footer.

#### User Story 1.1: Disclosure footer on the analysis page

Story ID: TBD

> As a GEO user, I want the analysis page to say which part is computed and which part is generated by AI, so that I know what I read.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Given any analysis view, When the page renders, Then the AI disclosure footer is the last element of the content column, after the Sources section, at full column width, with the long form
- Given a long page, When I scroll, Then the footer scrolls with the content and closes the page (it is not sticky)
- Given the Overview, a category view, the period view, a branded or unbranded analysis, or the Other category, When the page renders, Then the footer shows at the same place with the same text
- Given a page narrower than the text, When the footer renders, Then each part of the line (statement, separator, "Powered by AI Sense") wraps as a unit, never in the middle of "AI Sense"
- Given the InBrief perception summary on the page, When it renders, Then it keeps its own inline disclaimer as today, the footer does not replace it
- Given a widget of the page (Top Topics, Share of Voice, landscape table, Citation Sources), When it renders, Then it gets no disclosure of its own

#### User Story 1.2: Disclosure footer in the details panels

Story ID: TBD

> As a GEO user, I want the details panels to say that their content comes from AI, so that I read the answers and sources with the right expectation.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Given a details panel that shows AI data (Target Sources, Prompts analysis, Other type details, review pages), When I scroll to the end of the panel, Then the AI disclosure footer is the last element of the scrolling content, at full panel width, with the short form on one line
- Given the panel at rest, When the content is longer than the panel, Then the footer sits below the fold and shows only once I reach the end (it is not sticky)
- Given a panel with the source navigation bar (Previous, Source n of N, Next source), When I scroll, Then the bar stays pinned at the bottom edge and the footer never hides it
- Given a panel without an action bar, When I reach the end, Then the footer comes right after the content
- Given a panel that shows no AI data, When it renders, Then it has no footer

#### User Story 1.3: Disclosure line on the PowerPoint and PDF exports

Story ID: TBD

> As a GEO user who shares the export, I want every slide and page with AI data to carry the disclosure, so that the slide stays compliant when it is copied into another deck.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Given a PowerPoint export, When I open a content slide, Then its footer shows the export line centred between the analysis name (left) and the page number (right), at Caption 1 size, with "AI Sense" in Caption 1 Strong and the AI gradient, no icon and no hairline
- Given a long analysis name, When the slide is built, Then the line stays centred and the analysis name never clips (the footer row is three columns: analysis name, disclosure, page number)
- Given the cover slide, When I open it, Then it carries no disclosure line, as it shows no AI data
- Given a PDF export, When I open a content page, Then its page footer shows the same line at the same place as on the slide, and the cover page carries none
- Given an export in FR or ES, When I open it, Then the line shows in the export language

#### User Story 1.4: Disclosure line on every Excel sheet

Story ID: TBD

> As a GEO user who works on the Excel export, I want each sheet to say where its data comes from, so that a sheet copied on its own stays compliant.

Wireframe of every sheet, today and with the line: https://claude.ai/artifact/3BijZ4AeeU1wwaz3yJnQsY

Acceptance Criteria:

- Given an Excel export, When I open any sheet, Then the row right under the sheet title shows the export line, before any table, in italic grey like the sheet subtitles but at a smaller size (9 pt, the rest of the sheet is 11 pt)
- Given a sheet that already has a subtitle (Brand Pillars, Time Evolution), When I open it, Then the export line comes after the existing subtitle and before the empty row that opens the table
- Given the Raw Data sheet, which has no title, When I open it, Then the export line is on row 1, followed by one empty row, and the column header moves to row 3
- Given the Overview sheet, When the InBrief row shows, Then its label reads "InBrief (summary generated by artificial intelligence)" instead of "InBrief (AI summary)", in FR "InBrief (synthèse générée par intelligence artificielle)" instead of "InBrief (résumé IA)", in ES "InBrief (resumen generado por inteligencia artificial)" instead of "InBrief (resumen IA)"
- Given an export in FR or ES, When I open it, Then the line shows in the export language

#### User Story 1.5: Keep the InBrief attribution in the loading, empty and error states

Story ID: TBD

> As a GEO user, I want the InBrief attribution to stay while the summary loads or fails, so that the AI label never disappears.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Given the InBrief perception summary or the reputation drill-in panel, When it is loading, empty or in error, Then the InBrief header and "Powered by AI Sense" stay visible, as in the loaded state
- Given the summary fails, When the error state shows, Then it uses the approved copy: "Summary unavailable, please try again. Something went wrong while AI Sense was generating your summary. Give it another try, it usually works on the next attempt." with a retry button

#### User Story 1.6: Lite analysis wording and FR alignment

Story ID: TBD

> As a prospect on the lite analysis, I want the same AI statement as in the product, so that the disclosure is the same everywhere.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Given the lite analysis, When the InBrief summary shows, Then the statement reads "Summary generated by artificial intelligence" instead of "Summary generated by AI", in ES "Resumen generado por inteligencia artificial" instead of "Resumen generado por IA"
- Given the perception answers of the lite analysis, When they show, Then they carry the disclaimer under the answers
- Given the FR interface, the FR export and the FR lite analysis, When the AI statement shows, Then it reads "Synthèse générée par intelligence artificielle", the string of the analysis page, instead of "Résumé généré par intelligence artificielle" (export cover) and "Résumé généré par IA" (lite analysis)
